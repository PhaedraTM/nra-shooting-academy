About the Instructor

John Myseros is a certified NRA Instructor with more than 40 years of firearms experience and nearly two decades of teaching. Known for his clear explanations, calm instruction, and safety-first approach, John has trained shooters of all experience levels across Northern Virginia.

All classes emphasize safety, confidence, and responsible firearm ownership. Whether you're a brand-new shooter or seeking advanced training, our courses provide a hands-on, structured path to skill-building.
